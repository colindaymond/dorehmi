HOW TO PLAY

Use your voice

  1. Allow microphone access. Audio stays on your device.
  2. Start with DO in any octave. The game quietly locks that octave while you sing—there is no setup screen.
  3. Sing the target note. Your voice smoothly pans the magical scope up and down.
  4. Line up the bullseye. Too low sits below the deer; too high floats above it.
  5. Hold it on target. The friendly deer magically transforms into an antler rainbow—no bumps or boo-boos.
  6. Collect all eight rainbows to finish the melody.
